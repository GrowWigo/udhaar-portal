Have questions regarding Udhaar Portal loans? Here are answers to our most common inquiries directly from our lending portal.

Udhaar Portal is an online digital lending platform that provides fast, short-term unsecured personal loans ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹1,00,000. Loans are deployed in partnership with Focus Industrial Resources Limited, an RBI-regulated Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC CoR 14-01042), with Unifin Experts LLP serving as the designated Lending Service Provider (LSP).

You can borrow instant personal loans between ₹5,000 and ₹1,00,000. Approval limits are determined based on your monthly income, employment stability, and credit assessment using automated underwriting to ensure safe and responsible borrowing.

To qualify, applicants must be Indian citizens aged 21 years or older, possess a valid PAN card, have an active mobile number linked to Aadhaar for digital e-KYC, show proof of steady monthly employment or income, and hold a valid personal savings bank account.

The entire journey is 100% paperless and online. Complete our simple 2-minute application, submit your digital verification via DigiLocker/Aadhaar XML, and upon instant evaluation, review your transparent sanction letter. Once approved and digitally e-signed, funds are transferred directly to your bank account within 24 hours.

If you anticipate difficulty meeting an upcoming installment, contact our dedicated support team promptly at contact@udhaarportal.com or call 09311437690 before your due date. We can explore possible extension options, restructuring plans, or customized repayment arrangements to safeguard your credit profile.
